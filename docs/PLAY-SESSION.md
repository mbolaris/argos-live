# Product milestone: one complete AI build session

October 8, 2026. This defines the next experience milestone, not shipped behavior.
Extend [Mission 1](PROVING-GROUND-MISSION-1.md) and the shared foundations; do not
replace the event stream, scorers, result store, model catalog or workload owner.

The owner should finish a session thinking: **I saw what my AI did, understood
its weakness, tried an improvement, and know what to do next.**

## The activity

The game is building a dependable private assistant. The user is its builder;
the local AI is a participant with a voice. The shared early curriculum supplies
the next challenge. Users do not design benchmarks, choose a profession or shop
through a catalog before their first session. The blueprint and skill map carry
the giant-robot ambition; specific measured outcomes make progress meaningful.

Use one loop: **Challenge → Watch → Debrief → Try one change → Retest → Keep or
restore → Validate → Use it.** Every stage ends with one recommended available
action. The owner should be able to press one prominent **Go** control to follow
that next step; the adjacent heading explains exactly what Go will do. A stage
that cannot continue states the blocker and offers a useful exit. When the screen
discloses the exact bounded temporary trial, pressing Go is the owner's approval
to start that trial in one step. Go never automatically keeps a lasting assistant
change; the owner still chooses Keep or Restore after seeing its evidence.

## Release journey

1. **Boot and start.** One **GO** action is visible on phone and desktop, with
   an accessible name and a plain-language heading that say what it will do. Lead
   with the mission, what the AI will attempt and what the owner will see. Starter
   trials need no model storage or download. Existing full document qualification
   keeps its fixed suite and criteria; a future short introduction is practice,
   not a replacement suite or shortcut to qualification.
2. **Watch the attempt.** Give the current question and observable answer the
   main visual space. Completed receipts accumulate beside or below it. Progress,
   elapsed time, phase and Stop remain visible. Expand passages and old receipts
   on demand. Setup, speed and restoration get named phases, never invented
   challenge counts. Show no hidden reasoning. Reload and cancellation retain
   correct evidence and clear active indicators.
3. **Debrief together.** First show what passed and a representative miss. Explain
   correctness, response format and missing evidence separately. Then show the
   local model's bounded opinion with its model identity and an opinion label.
   It can acknowledge a weakness and suggest a next experiment; the independent
   controller validates what is available. Keep the measured receipt usable when
   the opinion fails. Put timings, all records and runtime details behind Details.
4. **Make one change.** Recommend one supported, reversible experiment with the
   exact change, reason and workload preview. The first inexpensive route is the
   reviewed concise Lab instruction preset (J6b); it changes the Lab recipe only,
   not the active assistant personality. Never pretend that Lab promotion improved
   the real OpenClaw agent. A suggestion needs approval and does not execute itself.
5. **Retest visibly.** Use the same watch screen. J7 validates one changed variable
   and matched model/recipe/suite/runtime/hardware/coverage before comparisons.
   Show changed answers and matched measures, not just a number. One extra answer
   is an observed gain, not confirmed general improvement. Keep/Restore makes the
   selected Lab recipe and its evidence explicit. Failure restores or explains
   recovery without claiming a successful improvement.
6. **Qualify the build.** Repeated visible questions are practice. Independent
   fixed validation and held-out original tasks are needed for stronger improvement
   claims. Fill only the supported task nodes for the current tested model/recipe.
   Absent validation is visibly pending; do not simulate it in the released app.
7. **Use the capability.** Offer the supported owner-pasted brief task, clearly
   owner-judged and distinct from scored exercises. Then recommend the next shared
   foundation. A failed candidate and a prudent rollback still move the activity
   forward by improving the owner's understanding.

## The skill map

Use one map rather than competing ladders, blueprints and category dashboards.
It has common foundation regions: following directions, reading, numbers,
reasoning/planning, sandboxed tools, integrated useful projects. Each region is
made of specific tasks with visible prerequisites; one small suite never fills
an entire domain. Professional specialization becomes a later branch.

Show three evidence states with both labels and marks: **Untested**, **Tested /
validation pending**, **Qualified**. Retain earlier model/recipe accomplishments
in history; distinguish them from the selected build. Let the map be explored,
but keep the current mission and next action dominant. The future robot stays
outlined without inventing an AGI percentage, intelligence rank or XP.

Qualification gets a quiet schematic change and the measured result. A first
commissioned capability gets a brief acknowledgement from the AI and a concrete
Use this action. Routine saves and downloads remain plain. Do not reward spending,
streaks, neglect or repeated tests as if they train the weights.

## Model upgrades and storage belong inside the loop

J3 carries the mission and source result into a stronger-model recommendation.
Show one reviewed candidate and why it may help, hardware fit uncertainty, download
size and the smaller/offline alternative. Do not promise the candidate is smarter
before testing or invent a tag that is not in the verified catalog.

Storage is an inline step with named destinations, free/needed space, encryption
and reboot behavior. Preview the exact new folder and perform the existing
explicit write check. Explain each unavailable destination; do not silently fall
back, adopt files, move a store, format a volume or change internal-disk permissions.
Paths and full data-location inventory belong in Details. S6's automount fix
restores eligibility; it does not complete this guided experience.

After verified download and explicit switch, return directly to the same mission.
Keep the starter and restoration route usable through refusal, offline operation,
cancellation, no space, failures and regressions.

## Implementation order and acceptance

- Complete J6b and J7 as reviewable slices, then integrate J8's focused watch,
  debrief and map into this whole session. J3 is the optional stronger-model route.
  SI1/SI2 add validated AI proposals and approved execution later; model opinion
  text is not an executable planner. J4 real tools and P8 identity remain separate.
- Track **session completion**, not number of panels implemented. A ready new
  owner should identify Start, watch an actual attempt, explain one miss, run the
  supported experiment, see a valid comparison, keep/restore, and find the next
  mission without commands, path entry or raw records. Require actual reviewer
  observation before claiming a usability pass.
- Validate at 390×844, 320px and desktop, including keyboard and reduced motion.
  Record actual rendered screenshots of ready, running, completed, comparison,
  blocked storage, cancelled and recovery states. No placeholder actions.
- Test the real backend recipe path and matched evidence rules. Separate fixture
  browser proof, real model output, shipped Firefox, OpenClaw behavior and physical
  GPU/persistence. A successful benchmark alone is not experience acceptance.
- Keep all existing time/token/heat/storage limits, privacy boundaries, pinned
  dependencies and USB deployment gates. Do not dispatch a new image until the
  integrated session is reviewable; deployment remains separately authorized.

Interactive design previews may use plainly labelled simulated answers and results.
They prove an interaction concept, not inference, speed, qualification or learning.
