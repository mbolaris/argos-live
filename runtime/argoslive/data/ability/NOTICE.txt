Argos Live original ability fixtures (each suite records its own version in JSON)

The quick (20 items), standard (70 items), challenge (40 items), tool-schema
(40 items), tool-plan (40 items), and tool-sequence (40 items) suites are
original Argos Live fixtures, not extracts from GSM8K, MMLU or another third-party dataset. Quick, standard and challenge
cover five deterministic formatting and answer categories, not a comprehensive
intelligence test. Quick is a subset of standard. Challenge is separately
authored. Tool-schema is a tool-call-only probe with 20 practice and 20 held-out
items; their tool names and argument keys are disjoint. Both are Argos-authored
forms, not external benchmarks or proof that real tools execute. Tool-plan adds
multi-constraint action selection and argument derivation; its bundled held-out
items are not independent validation. These forms are not matched replacements
for quick or standard. Tool-sequence asks for ordered two- or three-call plans
with explicit prior-result references; its bundled held-out workflows are an
internal transfer signal, not independent validation or a test of execution.
Challenge 1.2.0 makes the report-format argument in action-03 explicitly
lowercase. Challenge 1.1.0 remains available for replay; scores across versions
are not a matched before-and-after comparison.
Generated-code execution is omitted until an enforced sandbox is implemented.
