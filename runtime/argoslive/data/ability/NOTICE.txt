Argos Live original ability fixtures, version 1.0.0

The quick (20 items) and standard (70 items) suites are original Argos Live
fixtures, not extracts from GSM8K, MMLU or another third-party dataset. They cover
five basic formatting/ability categories, not a comprehensive intelligence test.
The quick suite is a subset of standard; they are not independent evaluations.
Generated-code execution is omitted until an enforced sandbox is implemented.
